# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Indoors by region: regions marked as floors under a roof keep rain and snow off (flat and 3D), stop sway and sun shadows there, and ease the darkness, lightning and the weather's sound while the player is inside.
- Bridges: deck and end regions give characters a level, so the player walks over and under the same bridge; characters on different levels pass and don't start each other; the level is saved.
- Doodads ride on the player or an event, or travel along points (loop, back and forth, once and saved), with their lights; carriers make their tiles walkable and take the player across water. Start Travel and Stop Travel.
- Ambient creatures: birds that fly off and come back (landing on Perch doodads), butterflies by day and fish under water, seeded, by hours and weather, scaled by Effects Quality; Add Sample Items draws a bird and a butterfly.
- Tile settings on the Tiles card: passage, sides, ladder, bush, counter, damage floor, boat, ship, airship and terrain tags as in MZ's database, one Undo step a click, saved to Tilesets.json with a backup and the changed-since check.
- Map Kinds: Edit Kind changes a kind's classes (name, walkable, height, region, biome, tile), adds and removes them, renames or deletes a kind; built-in kinds are copied first.
- Cutscene timelines (Map → Timelines): camera, states, lights, darkness, weather, looks, effects, characters, time, wind, sounds and switches on tracks against time, previewed in the editor and played by Play Timeline (wait, hold OK to skip) and Stop Timeline.
- Light shafts (a Screen Looks effect) from the sun or the brightest lamp through what stops light; Soft Light gives lamps soft shadow edges and glow off walls by Effects Quality.
- Decorate mode for players: a catalog of pieces and zones in Map → Decorate; Open Decorate Mode, Give Decor Item, Count Decor; pieces are kept in the save, can't wall the player in, and are handed back when the catalog or zone changes; released pieces that go are noted.
- A world that remembers: doodads that grow by calendar days, worn paths kept in the save, and footprints on marked ground and in snow.
- Fire and rain: fire spreads over marked dry ground with the wind, rain puts it out and leaves puddles, water runs downhill; Burns, Lights fires and Puts out doodads; Start Fire At and Put Out Fires.
- Map eras: one map in several versions picked by a switch or variable, crossfaded in the game, with onion skin, Copy Era and Delete Era in the editor.
- Rewind in a playtest: hold Ctrl+Backspace with the editor closed to go back up to 30 seconds (memory only).
- Places (Map → Places): make a place's map with transfers both ways, a tree of world, places and rooms with link checks, and a grid of maps joined as neighbours; new maps and MapInfos are written together with backups.
- Walking to neighbours: with neighbours linked and the game-wide switch on, the player walks off an edge onto the next map with no fade, the camera looking past linked edges and the neighbour's tiles drawn there.
- Bake This Map / Bake Every Map draws still doodads into a few pictures the game uses instead of their sprites while current; Problems and `npm run deploy -- --bake` report stale bakes.
- More Like This: learn a sample of the map and fill another region the same way (ground and doodads), previewed, rerolled and applied as one Undo step.
- From a Map File: an OpenStreetMap or GeoJSON file the user downloaded becomes roads, rivers, water, forests, buildings and bridges through Picture to Map, with the area's real size shown and the ODbL credit written to CREDITS_MAPS.txt; no download is made.
- Item Bag and Details can move to a window of their own (desktop playtest), keeping their keys and the game running.
- `npm run sample -- <new MZ project>` writes a sample town, field and cave with the plugin's own drawn art (docs/SAMPLE_PROJECT.md).
- Fix: previewing a time in the editor (the clock bar, the first-map quest's "Make it night", Find a Tool's "a rainy night") now shows night even while the Clock is Off, as it is in a new project. The game itself still follows the Clock setting.
- Fix: the first-map quest offers Add Sample Items while there are no doodad pictures (MZ's own files filled every new project's index, so it was never offered), and then opens the Item Bag on Doodads.
- Fix: the first-map quest card now shows for someone new. The quest started before the editor finished opening, so its card was never drawn and its steps never ticked off.
- Fix: with Select, the hint for a picked picture said "click the map to place it", but a click only picks. It now says to drag it onto the map or use Stamp.
- Fix: after editing, the window came back a frame too big (848 × 702 for 832 × 663). It now goes back to its exact size and place on Done and on F5.
- Fix: Item Bag and Details in a Window opened an empty window in MZ's NW.js 0.48 (the panel never moved), and once moved the Item Bag was squeezed to 100 pixels. The panel now moves when the window is ready and fills it.
- Fix: in the sample project the house door opens and shuts on OK, the flashlight shows only in the cave (switch 1), the cave is on the Dungeon tileset, and the data is written to `DocsDoodads.json` (a project with `DoodadsQJ.json` is refused too).
- Robot tester: Map → Test Every Map follows transfers from the start without playing and lists unreachable maps, dead ends, one-way trips, bad landings and variable transfers; Map → Test This Map also plays each reachable event on a copy of the game (messages answered, scene changes only noted, a frame limit) and restores the game. Findings have Go To.
- Map → Save 3D Map as File writes the ground (terrain or a flat quad) with the map's tiles as its picture and standing doodads as upright pictures to one .glb in screenshots/, after a dialog that notes the art's licences. A pure glTF writer is read back by the plugin's own reader in tests.
- Map → Picture to Map: a drawing becomes tiles, regions, heights and biome art after agreeing a colour key, as one undo step. Four kinds of map (Overworld, Town or castle, Dungeon or cave, Room) shape the plan: coasts and place markers, buildings with doors facing streets and gates, walls/floors/doors/stairs and rough cave walls, and room outlines. Thin lines can be ignored, a grey picture gives heights, tiles per class are remembered per tileset, and copied kinds travel in packs.
- Map → Ground Brush paints up to three repeating textures with soft edges over the ground tiles and under doodads; right-drag erases and each stroke is one undo step keeping only its rectangle. The mask is saved as a PNG beside the art (none when empty), deployed with its textures, and repeated across looping seams.
- Movement off the grid (World → Movement, off by default): the player moves by pixels in eight directions with a feet box that slides along painted walls, doodad bodies, impassable tiles, tile edges and character events, with corner nudging and sub-steps. The player's tile stays whole for events and plugins; each new tile is a step (party walk, floor events, encounters). Touch events, the action button, followers on a trail and touch destinations work; events, vehicles, forced routes, jumps and traversal stay on the grid.
- Map → Import Map makes a new MZ map from a Tiled (`.tmj`/`.json`) or LDtk (`.ldtk`) file: tile layers become map-locked picture layers (behind or over the map by the Player layer), tile objects become doodads with cut pictures, collision layers become painted collision, and tile sizes are scaled. Flips, external tilesets/levels and base64/zlib data are read; pictures are written all-or-nothing and MapInfos is backed up. A new map setting, Empty tiles are floor, makes tileless picture maps walkable.
- Moving tooltips: the ten main tools' tooltips play a short drawn clip of the tool in use after a moment (never with Less motion). The clips are drawn by `scripts/tool_clips.js` into an editor-only part of about 19 KB; verify checks their pixels and a 48 KB budget.
- Pins and fast travel: right-click Add Pin Here (name, icon, switch, travel) or `<docs pin: icon, name>` on an event page. Found pins (explored tile, nearby, or Find Pin) show on the minimap and world map; the world map travels to found travel pins after a confirmation unless the Forbid Fast Travel Switch is ON. Find Pin, Hide Pin and Open Fast Travel commands; found/hidden ids are saved; Problems flags unreachable travel pins; crash recovery keeps pins.
- Ropes and cables: Path → Draw a Rope Instead spans two points with a sagging line, plain or textured with the picked picture, that sways with the wind (ends fixed). Details sets sag, thickness, colour and sway, and hangs evenly spaced pictures with optional light presets. End and sag handles are undoable; hung art deploys and follows Rename/Move. Plain ropes store the empty icon so older builds show nothing.
- View → Phone Preview serves the playtest read-only to a phone on the same Wi-Fi from a QR code, after asking. It binds to a private network address with a random token, serves only game folders (never saves, backups or reports), answers GET/HEAD with ranges, and reloads the phone where the player stood when data is saved. Stop, closing the editor or the playtest stops it; the game build leaves it out.
- Doodads without an `_n.png` get a normal map made from their shape: rounded from the edge of the opaque art plus brightness detail, per frame with no bleeding, made in slices during playtests and cached in `img/doodads/_normals/`. Details → Shading offers Made for me, From file or Off with Strength and Rounded/Flat; hand-made files win, edited pictures are remade, and deployment restores only maps for pictures in use.
- A chosen flat map can run behind the title menu, with fixed time, season, weather, look and a looping camera path. Title update/render uses an isolated game context and renderer caches; starting a new game keeps its own state.
- Find a Tool understands offline phrases such as “make it a rainy night”, fog, darker and bigger. It lists the exact commands before Enter; unknown/conflicting words run nothing. Translations can supply aliases, weather stays a preview, and authored changes use Undo.
- Playtest trails count tile entries, standing game frames and Game Overs locally. View → Show Trails adds a bounded heat overlay, stops/deaths with Go To, JSON merging and Clear. Desktop tester builds can opt in; ordinary player builds do not record or write, and nothing is uploaded.
- Optional save-file pictures reuse MZ's map snapshot as a JPEG under 8 KB, with area name and game time. Native rows handle old, missing and unreadable pictures; stale snapshots are cleared on returning to the map or loading another game.
- Flat maps can supply lit, unblurred battle backgrounds. Shared capture hides characters, gear, shadows, reflections and HUD elements temporarily; named battlebacks win and capture failures keep MZ's fallback.
- Events can see the player through a light-sensitive cone stopped by lighting walls. Details edits note/page tags and previews the cone; lightAt/sees APIs, Store Light Level and a configurable darkness switch support puzzles. Gameplay checks remain independent of visual quality.
- Picture placement previews use the real map sprite, depth, animation, light, shadow and reflection, with a twelve-sprite limit for Scatter, Paint and Path. Brush random sequences stay unchanged; cleanup removes previews and their light/reflection contributions from captures without advancing game fades.
- GIF and APNG files become bounded PNG frame sheets with timing and exact frame counts ready for placement. Disposal, transparency and common PNG colour types are decoded locally; source files survive even a same-path overwrite choice. Metadata travels through the real drawer drag and packs. Unsupported 16-bit/Adam7 APNGs receive a clear error.
- Climate rolls reproducible weather spans from the day, season and saved seed. World tables set rain/storm/snow/fog/wind chances and duration; indoor maps suppress it, event weather holds until Resume Climate, and the clock strip, forecast API and Store Forecast report coming days.
- The 3D map can use a first-person camera at terrain-relative eye height, with eased quarter-turns, forward/back steps, optional click-to-lock mouse look and near-object fading. The player is hidden while followers stay visible; the editor borrows orbit view, and Photo Mode releases mouse look.
- Art credits can be recorded per file or inherited from a folder, searched by author/licence, carried through packs and file moves, and written to CREDITS.txt for referenced art with the LPC section. Supplied readme/licence text is offered as a note; missing-credit Problems are opt-in.
- New picture placements can start with conservative lamp, tree, bush, rock, building, fence, water, fire or sign settings. Saved defaults win; Details remembers or changes the kind, Settings turns guessing off, and Undo settings keeps the picture in place.
- Player Photo Mode pauses the map while composing a picture, with bounded camera controls, looks, Hide Player and PNG saving. Desktop pictures stay under screenshots; browser games download them. Leaving or a capture failure restores camera, look and visibility.
- Effects Quality adds Auto with delayed, eased frame-time decisions and a longer wait before raising detail. It remembers the settled level, respects fixed choices, and fades between quality levels; Start players on Auto is off by default.
- Generate Map builds seeded dungeons or wilderness in the editor with preview/reroll and one-step undo, or before a runtime transfer from a saved seed/recipe. Room openings, biome tiles, dressing and required art work in the game-only build.
- Heat haze bends flat-map air over campfires and lava, across a desert-road band or under water. It keeps the nearest eight visible sources, respects quality and reduced camera motion, and stays out of stitched map exports.
- Mark as Released records names, states and map sizes. Later renames/deletions can keep old save names through undoable aliases; Problems and deployment highlight changed release data. New state changes keep names while older numeric saves still read.
- Flat maps can shade doodads and characters from `_n.png` companions and nearby lamps. Normal maps follow animated frames, art moves and deployment; a generator supplies a rough starting map. Quality controls the light count; real GPU performance validation is pending.
- New installs get a five-step first-map quest with observed progress, control hints, sample art and remembered Skip/Not Now. How to Use and Find a Tool can replay it.
- Motion and lamps can follow shared Bass, Middle or Treble bands from BGM; particles can burst on a kick. The analyser is lazy, ignores sound effects and is released when unused. `api.beat()` exposes the bands.
- Problems checks plugin order and observes bypassed update hooks. Info saves a local report with bounded warnings/errors, project-relative paths and only this plugin's parameters. No report is uploaded; compatibility playtests remain pending.
- Event Details edits pages, conditions and common commands, including sign text and door destinations picked on another map. Unknown commands and branch markers are preserved; each change is undoable.
- Details offers a short list for pictures, lights, sounds, models and events, with More and a search across hidden settings. Pins stay visible; settings used often rise in the list using counts kept on this computer.
- Events can follow clock and weekday schedules, walk to a destination, hide between visits and pause for cutscenes. Details edits note or active-page schedules with undo and a tile picker.
- Trace a Picture fits an editor-only image under the grid, with move, scale, turn, opacity, locking and a two-point fit to tiles. Fits and drags undo in one step; the picture stays out of play, captures, map exports and deployed art.
- Simple mode starts new installs with Select, Stamp, Scatter, Tiles and shorter Details. Show Everything restores the full editor, while Ctrl+K and shortcuts reach every tool in either mode; existing editor preferences keep the full view.
- Collision brush: paint or erase fine cells, join lines, fill an enclosed space, or read collision from a picture layer. Each stroke is one undo step; walls travel with split map data.
- Character creators accept a My parts folder, keep clashing names separately and offer unknown families in an Extra tab. Recipes remember which folder supplied a part.
- Map → Moods tries bundled light, looks and ambience on hover and keeps them with one undo step. Weather, wind and the clock are previews; save your own moods as presets.

## 2.0.0-alpha.1 — 2026-10-19

- Fixes from the release checklist: the top bar fits the window at any editor size (at 125% and 150% in MZ's 816 × 624 window, More, Save and Done were off its edge): the words under the tools go first, then it takes a second row. Games without Aseprite gear no longer log a failed request for `data/DocsDoodadsAseprite.json` on every start (desktop games look for it first).
- Templates, presets and saving: More → Templates renames, re-sorts and deletes your own event templates (saving one under a name in use asks first), and packs carry them. More → Map Settings copies some or all of another map's settings (one map's ground only if asked) as one undo step, and keeps settings as presets. Ctrl+click and Shift+click pick several items in the Item Bag to tag, favourite, add to a collection, move to another folder (updating what uses them) or delete if unused (into the editor's bin). Steps keeps named checkpoints of a map through Save and closing, restored as one undo step. Save This Map saves one map, and Autosave (More) saves every 5, 10 or 20 minutes of unsaved work. Packs bring in a zip of art, or a pack inside a zip, with the same checks on where files may go.
- Finding and picking: Find (More, or the palette) searches this map or all maps by picture, Name, `tag:`, `name:`, `layer:`, `has:` (light, sound, collision, states, touch, particles) and `is:` (locked, hidden, conditional), a leading - leaving out; Pick All, Hide, Show, Lock and Unlock act on all it found as one undo step. The Outliner lists this map's doodads by layer with Hide, Lock and Solo per row. Ctrl+drag picks what the box touches and Alt+drag draws a lasso; "Pick doodads in a region..." picks those standing in a region. With several picked: Snap to Grid, rows or columns some pixels apart, and Ctrl+arrows move them a tile where the ground lets them stand.
- Movement, collision, light and camera: a doodad's collision can stop only the player or only events, be crossed one way (a ledge), and change shape with its state (an open gate keeps its posts). Events swim with `<docs swim>` and fly with `<docs fly>`; taking off, landing and diving can run common events, landing can be kept below a terrain height, and move routes can `this.docsJump()`. Clicking where the player can't go shows a cross; clicking a touchable doodad walks up and touches it; each map can have its own click marker. Doodads with collision and events marked `<block light>` (doors) stop light; a light can need a switch or a condition. 2D Camera Follow → Nothing now holds the camera while the player moves; Zoom to Point zooms on a tile or a character. In 3D, doodads beyond "Plain beyond" tiles are drawn without shadows or effects. Each save writes the files the plugin draws to `data/DocsDoodadsRequired.json`, and `npm run deploy -- --restore` puts them back into a deployment made with "Exclude unused files".
- For events: a calendar on the running clock (day 1, 2, 3..., weekdays by name, named dates, day and weekday variables, the Calendar command, `api.day()` and friends); the clock can run in battles and menus, and a battle can take some minutes. A state can play a sound and an effect when a doodad goes into it and go back after some seconds (a trap that resets); Set Doodad State can wait for the crossfade. A touch can run only once (the save remembers), wait between touches, and show a prompt (Open, Read, !) while the player faces it. Play Doodad Animation plays a strip once, now, with `animationEnd` for scripts. Explored Map reveals a region and puts how much is explored in a variable. `api.place` and `api.remove` take a transition, and removing no longer rebuilds the map's other doodads. The API covers the plugin commands: `touch`, `resetTouch`, `playAnimation`, `setWind`, `flashlight`, `traversal`, `traversalAction`, `playVfx`, `reveal`, `explored`, `feature`.
- Project and release: one version (2.0.0-alpha.1) in package.json, the plugin header, the editor and the changelog, checked by `npm run verify` along with What's New (new items for this version), the translation template (`npm run strings -- --check`) and ESLint (`npm run lint`). Each test suite runs in a folder of its own and verify fails if one changes the repository. The coverage gate is 85/85/80/75 (91 % of lines covered), Cut Up Sheet and Save as PNGs have a suite of their own, the suites' time budgets follow the machine's speed, and CI runs them once per system. The guide lists Doodad JSON Formatting, the cheat sheet `[bN]` and `[hN]`, SECURITY.md says that doodad conditions are code, README and the header describe More's twenty-odd entries, and CONTRIBUTING.md has a release checklist. `.gitignore` covers what the plugin writes into a project.
- Problems lists doodads whose condition isn't JavaScript that runs (they always show), with Remove Condition.
- Fixes: undoing a delete of several doodads puts them back in their places (picked out of order, they came back reordered); undoing a placement after going to another map no longer puts the doodad there; undoing the delete of an event no longer shows one the game had erased; the drag ghost no longer reloads its picture on every mouse move; + and − are only taken from the game while zooming by keys is on.
- A quicker editor. The crash snapshot is written only when something changed since the last one, without holding up the editor (a save meanwhile wins). The Item Bag's library is written a moment after a change (a favourite, tags, a collection, collision) and on Save or closing, compact (about 40 % smaller). Key presses no longer read the saved keys for every shortcut. Where-used reads a map or database file again only when it changed on disk. Bringing in files indexes those files instead of every folder (here 0.5 s instead of 12 s), a dropped folder is walked once, and same-sized files are compared a piece at a time. The map panel's list of doodad pictures is filled when a field wants it, the packing note is checked every few seconds, thumbnails are read from disk in the background and the cache keeps one per item (up to 3,000), and Save backs up only the files it changes.
- Faster maps with many doodads. Doodads off the screen (or off the 3D view) are not drawn, and their shadows, filters and effects wait; their lights still shine. On a 200 × 200 map with 3,000 doodads and 375 lights this took Show Speed from 23 to 60 frames a second on the flat map (12,000 draw calls a frame to 150) and from 14 to 60 in 3D (24,000 to 248). Per doodad and frame, the game no longer rebuilds the layer list, the season, the filter signature or a light's settings; a character's step checks the doodads on that tile instead of all of them; light masks are traced once and moved with the map; post-processing, visual equipment and the ambience around the player are worked out again only when what they depend on changes; characters' frames no longer make a new texture each time (they were never let go).
- Memao creator: the export stands characters on the tile's bottom (every frame 6 pixels lower, as MZ's own characters; on at first, a sheet that would lose pixels stays as it was and is named), and a pack with more than one prop of a kind gets a row in Tools to choose it.
- MZ creator: a part offers every colour slot its walking and battler pictures use, not only its face's (108 parts gain one, such as a cloak's second colour).
- Character creators: Export recipe... and Import recipe... (under Load) keep one character in a file of its own anywhere on the computer; a recipe from another creator says which to switch to.
- Character creators: a list beside the window's title switches between the libraries scanned (MZ, LPC, Memao), keeping the name and each library's character. The palette has two entries, Characters (the card) and Character Creator... (the one used last), in place of four. The Characters card has a group per library that stays folded or open as left. The export picks the face slot from the face sheet's eight faces as they are now. Under High contrast the window is black on white with 3-pixel borders.
- Character creators: a padlock on each row (or L) keeps it through the dice (an MZ part with its colours). The body dice keep the body type and give a new face and hair (the LPC ones only ever gave male or female, losing a chosen teen, child, muscular or pregnant body); Shift+X also rolls the type, among those the library has and the locked parts fit. Ctrl+Z goes back a step, the last 20. On a gamepad: hold X for a new type, Y with a direction locks, Back with B undoes (X, Y and Back alone now act when let go).
- Memao creator: long hair, side hair and eyebrows take the hair's colour, shown by a chain in place of their swatch; click it (or K) for a colour of their own, and again to link back.
- Character creators: click a row's value (or press Enter on the row) for all its choices in a grid of pictures, None first and the one worn marked. Typing finds one by name, the arrows move, Enter picks, Esc clears what was typed and then closes; on a gamepad the right stick opens it, the d-pad moves, A picks and B closes.
