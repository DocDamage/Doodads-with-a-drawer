## Water outside the 3D ground capture

After PRs #97 and #98 were merged into `54ba99a`, the water artifact was reproduced and corrected on `codex/water-capture-bounds`. RED checkpoint: `969b40d`; GREEN checkpoint: `cb6851d`.

### Cause and correction

The terrain margin and flat ground plane can extend beyond their finite captured texture. On looping maps the water mask wrapped those outside coordinates back onto water, adding animated highlights over the void. The shared water shader now checks the original capture UV before wave distortion, shading or highlights. The in-bounds wave, shoreline and wake calculations are unchanged. Both flat and terrain ground shaders use the corrected block.

### Real WebGL regression

`scratch/qa_water_capture.js` compiles the production water GLSL on a separate offscreen WebGL 1 canvas in MZ/NW.js. An all-water looping mask, UVs crossing the left/right capture boundaries, two forced animation times and pixel readback test the visible result without changing the map. Run it in a disposable playtest's DevTools console as shown in the helper's header.

| Measurement | Before fix | After fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outside samples | 4,096 | 4,096 |
| Outside samples with incorrect void colour | 2,289 | 0 |
| Outside samples changing between water times | 2,694 | 0 |
| Inside samples changing between water times | 7,996 of 8,192 | 7,996 of 8,192 |

The failing probe ran before the shader change; the same probe passed afterwards. This is a GPU rendering check of the actual production shader chunk, rather than a CPU duplicate or shader-text assertion. It requires a real WebGL/MZ environment and is separate from the normal Node suites.
