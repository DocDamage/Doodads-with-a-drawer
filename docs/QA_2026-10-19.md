## Not covered

- **A real lower-spec PC (B)** and **watching a new user (E)**: they need a different computer and a person. Nothing here stands in for them.
- **A physical gamepad**: the pad was simulated through the browser's Gamepad API, which is what MZ reads.
- **Screen readers**: names and roles are set and checked in tests; Narrator itself was not run.
- **Doodad packs between two real projects in MZ**: covered by `test_doodad_packs` with two sandbox projects and real files, not in a playtest.
- **The MZ editor reopening a map painted with Tiles**: the tiles are written into the map file the same way as region painting (checked in tests), but the MZ editor wasn't opened on the result.
